Why this box, not a PIQ: readers see six colleges on the Academic History and 4 colleges in one summer. One plain factual note answers "why" in the place they look, so PIQ #4 can stay a story instead of a transcript explanation. It names only courses already on your record and the three Spring 2027 courses that matter most for EE prep.

### Applicant must confirm before pasting
1. CVC: did you actually enroll through the California Virtual Campus (cvc.edu) Exchange? If not, use Version 2. Do not mention CVC unless it is true.
2. The reason: the note says FLC had few sections, waitlisted labs, and times that clashed with your job, and that the normal path would have taken 3-4 years (from [[piq-beat-sheets]]). If cost, online availability, or another reason also applied, add it in plain words and recount. The reason must be yours.
3. "Los Rios" for CISP 360 (Summer 2026) and Spring 2027: the course table says Los Rios, not FLC. If any of these were at a different Los Rios college (for example American River or Cosumnes River), the note should name it, because UC will see that college on your record too.
4. "Finishes my calculus-based physics sequence": confirm on ASSIST that Physics 411, 421, and 431 make up the full sequence for your target campuses (same flag as the required PIQ).
5. Spring 2027 also includes Chem 401, ENGR 312, and ENGL C1000. They are left out to save space and because the planned-coursework section already lists them. Add them only if you have characters to spare.
6. Earlier terms: [[courses-and-majors]] lists coursework only from Spring 2026 on. If you have earlier FLC terms (likely, given the 3.97 FLC GPA and Honor List), nothing in Box A needs to change, but make sure every college you attended is listed on the Academic History, or UC can treat it as an omission.
7. Grades: Fall 2026 is in progress and Spring 2027 is planned. Report both on the Transfer Academic Update by Jan 31, 2027.

## Box B - PIQ: Additional Comments (550 words max)

**Recommendation: LEAVE BLANK.**

Why: the gaps a reader could have are already covered.
- Multi-college path and the summer course load: Box A (facts) plus PIQ #4 (the story).
- Spring 2027 major prep not yet taken: Box A and the planned-coursework section of the application.
- Work hours next to the course load: Activity #2 and PIQ #1.
- Choice of EE and readiness: the required PIQ.
- Activity hours: fixed by labeling in [[uc-activities-350char]], not by explanation.

Nothing left is a real gap. Filling Box B with a restatement of the PIQs would cost reader attention and add nothing they cannot already see.

When to use it instead: only if a fact the reader needs is true and not in your data, such as a family, health, or financial circumstance that shaped the multi-college path or your hours. That is yours to write in 80-120 plain words. Nothing like that appears in `00-context/`, so no draft is given here, and none should be invented.
