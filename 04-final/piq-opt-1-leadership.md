# Optional PIQ #1 Leadership - FINAL
Prompt: Describe an example of your leadership experience in which you have positively influenced others, helped resolve disputes or contributed to group efforts over time.

"Where's my food?"

The customer was leaning over the counter. It was a Friday dinner rush at Jack in the Box, and our newest hire had frozen at the register, her finger over the wrong button. I'd trained her on that screen myself.

I stepped in beside her and voided the entry. "Combo first, then the drink. I'll take the next one." She finished the order.

Rushes broke us in a worse way, too. Whenever ticket times spiked, the counter and the kitchen turned on each other. Cashiers blamed the cooks for being slow. Cooks blamed us for sending orders in wrong. One night the grill cook yelled, "Fix your screen before you send me garbage." Nobody fixed anything. We just got slower.

I was Shift Trainer for four new hires, and when I heard them start blaming the kitchen too, I changed how I trained them. POS and food safety weren't enough. I had each hire ring practice orders, then watch their own tickets come up on the line, so they could see what one missed "no onions" did to the grill. Before every peak, I staged cups and sauces. I started callouts between stations: "Big order coming." When a bottleneck hit, I moved to the worst spot and kept my voice level.

The grill cook ignored my callouts for weeks. I wanted to cover every station myself. Instead, I asked him what was breaking his station.

"Everything hits me at once."

So we made a deal. The counter would call out large orders before ringing them in. Once he could see a rush coming, he stopped yelling at the register.

Within a few months, my new hires were making callouts without being told, and they ran the front counter through Friday rushes without me stepping in. Leading that shift didn't mean fixing every order myself. It meant getting everyone ready before the rush hit, so the counter and the grill were working on the same order instead of against each other.
