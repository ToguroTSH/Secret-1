# Optional PIQ #4: Educational Opportunity or Barrier - FINAL
Prompt: Describe how you have taken advantage of a significant educational opportunity or worked to overcome an educational barrier you have faced.

At Folsom Lake College's pace, my engineering prerequisites would have taken three to four years. Math and physics ran in only a few sections, labs filled with waitlists, and the open times clashed with each other or with my 12 hours a week at Jack in the Box. If I missed one seat, every course behind it would slip a full year.

So I built the schedule myself. FLC Transfer Center workshops showed me how ASSIST articulation works, and then I mapped every requirement on it one course at a time. When FLC had no seat that fit my week, I searched other community colleges for sections that would transfer. Before registering, I logged each course, college, term, and articulation match in one spreadsheet.

That plan took me to six colleges. In Summer 2026 I was enrolled at four of them at once, taking eight courses. I put Calc 3 and Differential Equations with Linear Algebra first on purpose, because Physics 411 and ENGR 420 came next and both run on that math.

Some nights I had four course portals open and a shift the next day. I doubted I could keep my grades up.

The topics I wanted after physics, aerodynamics and propulsion, sat outside what community colleges offer. Over breaks I worked through MIT OpenCourseWare modules in both and used the supplementary problem sets to connect my physics and differential equations to aerospace problems.

Other students were stretched thin too, and I needed structure as much as they did. I organized the FLC STEM Study Group so six to eight of us in Calc 3, Linear Algebra, Physics 411, and Chem 400 could meet for three hours every week to trade condensed formula sheets, work problem sets on whiteboards, and keep each other on track before lab practicals and finals.

My grades held. I have about 74 units and a 3.97 GPA, and I made the President's and Dean's Honor List every semester. This fall I started Physics 411 and ENGR 420 with the math already finished.
