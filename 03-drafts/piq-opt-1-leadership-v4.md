# Optional PIQ #1 Leadership - v4 (voice pass)
Prompt: Describe an example of your leadership experience in which you have positively influenced others, helped resolve disputes or contributed to group efforts over time.

"Where's my food?"

A customer was leaning over the counter during a Friday dinner rush at Jack in the Box, and our newest hire had frozen at the register with her finger over the wrong button. I had trained her on that screen myself. I stepped in next to her, voided the bad entry, and said, "Combo first, then the drink. I'll take the next one." She finished the order.

Whenever ticket times spiked, the front counter and the kitchen turned on each other. Cashiers said the cooks were slow, and the cooks said we sent orders in wrong. One night the grill cook yelled, "Fix your screen before you send me garbage." Nobody fixed anything, and we just got slower.

I was the Shift Trainer for four new hires, and when I heard them start blaming the kitchen too, I changed how I trained. POS and food safety were still the basics, but I had each hire ring practice orders and then watch their own tickets come up on the line, so they could see what a missed "no onions" did to the grill. Before every peak I staged cups and sauces. I started short callouts between stations, like "Big order coming," and when a bottleneck hit, I moved to the worst spot and kept my voice level.

The grill cook ignored the callouts for weeks. Part of me wanted to just cover every station myself. Instead I asked him what was breaking his station, and he said, "Everything hits me at once." So we made a deal that the counter would call out large orders before ringing them in. Once he could see a rush coming, he stopped yelling at the register.

Within a few months, my new hires were making callouts without being told and running the front counter through Friday rushes without me stepping in. Most of what I did as a trainer happened before the rush ever started, making sure each person knew what their order looked like by the time it hit the grill.
