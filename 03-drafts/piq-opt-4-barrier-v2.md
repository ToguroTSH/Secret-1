# Optional PIQ #4: Educational Opportunity or Barrier - v2
Prompt: Describe how you have taken advantage of a significant educational opportunity or worked to overcome an educational barrier you have faced.

At Folsom Lake College's pace, my engineering prerequisites would have taken three to four years. Math and physics ran in only a few sections, labs filled with waitlists, and the open times clashed with each other or with my 12 hours a week at Jack in the Box. Missing one seat would push every course behind it back a full year.

So I built the schedule myself. FLC Transfer Center workshops showed me how ASSIST articulation works, and then I mapped every requirement on it one course at a time. Where FLC had no seat that fit my week, I searched other community colleges for sections that would transfer. I tracked each course, college, term, and articulation match in one spreadsheet before registering.

The plan took me to six colleges. The hardest stretch was Summer 2026: four colleges at once and eight courses. I put Calc 3 and Differential Equations with Linear Algebra at the front on purpose, because Physics 411 and ENGR 420 were next and both run on that math.

Some nights I had four course portals open and a shift the next day. I doubted I could keep my grades up.

There was a second limit. The topics I wanted after physics sat outside what community colleges offer, so over breaks I worked through MIT OpenCourseWare modules in aerodynamics and propulsion, and used the supplementary problem sets to connect my physics and differential equations to aerospace problems.

I was not the only one stretched thin, and I needed structure as much as anyone. I organized the FLC STEM Study Group so 6-8 of us in Calc 3, Linear Algebra, Physics 411, and Chem 400 could meet for three hours every week, trade condensed formula sheets, work problem sets on whiteboards, and keep each other accountable before lab practicals and finals.

The plan held. I have about 74 units and a 3.97 GPA, and the grades I doubted earned me the President's and Dean's Honor List every semester. This fall I started Physics 411 and ENGR 420 with the math already finished.
