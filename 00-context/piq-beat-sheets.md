## 1. Required PIQ: Major Preparation
- Prompt: "Please describe how you have prepared for your intended major, including your readiness to succeed in your upper-division courses once you enroll at the university."
- Angle: Bridging classroom theory to physical/computational debugging.
- Beats:
  - Hook (~50w): 2-axis gimbal oscillating wildly on the bench at FLC's Innovation Center despite the code compiling cleanly.
  - Coursework-to-build bridge (~120w): Calc 3, Diff Eq/Linear Algebra, Physics 411 (in progress), and self-studied controls applied to the 4th-order Runge-Kutta orbital sim, STM32 C++ PID tuning (15+ bench tests), and KiCad/ESP32 oscilloscope noise debugging (50Hz logging).
  - Industry validation (~100w): Shashine Technologies Python automation scripts and QA edge-case debugging.
  - Upper-division readiness (~80w): ready for upper-division controls, circuits, dynamics, and lab-heavy courses. May name Spring 2027 ENGR 400 / Physics 421 as upcoming, not completed.

## 2. Optional PIQ #1: Leadership

- Angle: Jack in the Box Shift Trainer, leading under real pressure.
- Beats:
  - Hook (~50w): Friday dinner rush, backed-up drive-thru, frustrated customer, new hire freezing on the POS register.
  - Conflict (~80w): recurring friction between front counter and kitchen when ticket times spiked; blame; slower output. (Answers "resolve disputes".)

  - Action (~150w): biweekly Fusion 360, Arduino breadboarding, and Python workshops in Aspen Hall FL1-130; helping 3 teams prep for the FLC Tech Summit; mentoring 10-15 middle schoolers at Folsom Public Library and FRC/VEX robotics students.
